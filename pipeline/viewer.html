SketchUp Free: import the STL (choose Meters). SketchUp Pro/Go: the KMZ keeps the photo texture; DAE works too. Points: PLY or LAS for Scan Essentials / CloudCompare. STL, DAE, KMZ and LAS are Z-up with north +Y; OBJ, GLB and PLY are Y-up like the viewer. Only PLY, OBJ and GLB keep vertex colours. Files are built in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and contain no real-world coordinates.

Drop a .ply file on the page to view it locally — nothing is uploaded.
